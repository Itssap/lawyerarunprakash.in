About Arun Prakash

Arun Prakash is an Advocate enrolled with the Bar Council of Tamil Nadu & Puducherry, practicing before the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India since 2018.

With focused expertise in criminal law and appellate litigation, he has represented clients in serious criminal matters including capital offence cases before the Supreme Court of India.

Originally from Tamil Nadu, he brings a deep understanding of the legal landscape of South Indian courts, while practicing at the apex court level in New Delhi. He is also involved in legal research, digital legal tools, and Tamil legal documentation.
