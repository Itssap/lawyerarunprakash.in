Advance Payment Policy

An advance of ₹500 or 50% of the tentative total fee — whichever is higher — is required before commencement of translation work. The balance is payable upon delivery of the translated document. The tentative fee will be communicated after reviewing the document and confirming the translated page count.

💡 How page count works: All translated documents are delivered in A4 size — the standard format for Supreme Court of India filings. 200 words in the translated output = 1 page. If the remainder after full pages exceeds 100 words, it is counted as an additional page; if 100 words or fewer, it is not charged. You will be informed of the exact page count and total fee before work begins.
